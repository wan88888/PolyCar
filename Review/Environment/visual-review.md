# Visual review — ten route environments

Reviewed the ten `route-XX-driving-sheet.png` files (four fixed driver-height views per route, 40 views total) and all ten overview images in `all-route-overviews.png`. This is visual inspection of static editor renders, not human playtesting, a continuous driving video, or target-device performance certification. The separate environment probes and deterministic driving reports cover other checks.

| Route | Observed composition and visibility |
|---|---|
| 01 Harbor | Container yard, cranes, ship and waterfront distinguish the port. Road edges, gates and turning direction remain visible; no obvious scenery clipping or shadow artifacts in the sampled views. |
| 02 Works district | Warehouses, tanks, pipes and service yards frame the S bends. Drain grates sit outside the line. Broad corner exits and gates remain clear. |
| 03 Forest climb | Sloping pine terrain, guardrails, distant mountains and the lookout visible in the overview establish the mountain forest. The selected climbing and switchback views retain a clear road corridor. |
| 04 Quarry | Stepped quarry benches, rubble piles and construction equipment are visible from the course and overview. Road shoulders and guardrails read cleanly; no visible floating rock or camera penetration in these captures. |
| 05 Sunset coast | Warm sunset sky/light, long palm shadows, coastal water and pavilion create a distinct silhouette. Dark asphalt remains separable from the sand; gates and arrows stay visible. |
| 06 Fishing village | Seaside houses and lighthouse are visible from the route; the overview also shows the timber pier and moored boats. The broad inner parcels remain deliberately open, and not every sampled driving angle sees the pier. No observed route obstruction. |
| 07 Red canyon | Red faceted walls and buttes surround the route. The narrow visual enclosure still leaves clear shoulders, gate positions and bend exits in the sampled views. |
| 08 Alpine pass | Climbing hairpins, valley slopes and snow-capped distant peaks are visible. Guardrails define the drop-side edges. No obvious clipping or distracting shadow defects in the selected turns. |
| 09 Heavy industry | Large storage tanks, chimney group and raised orange pipelines establish the refinery; the overview confirms their arrangement. Interior industrial parcels remain spacious. The course and obstacle approaches remain readable. |
| 10 Night streets | Correctly oriented neon lettering, lit windows, a dark blue sky and localized warm streetlight pools establish night. The car, edge lines, arrows and gate beacons remain distinguishable through lit and unlit sections. |

No blocking visual defect was found in the reviewed captures. The sampled daytime vehicle/scenery shadows show no obvious acne or large detachment. Night streetlights intentionally use shadowless local lights; the directional light supplies the shared shadow treatment. Static screenshots cannot establish temporal stability, moving-camera clearance at every driving angle, collision behavior or frame pacing.

Overview images retain each environment's atmospheric fog, so the forest and sunset overview contrast is lower than the route cards. The route-card capture pipeline disables fog for map readability. Further visual density in the fishing village/refinery inner parcels is an art-polish option, not an observed obstruction or missing core theme asset.
